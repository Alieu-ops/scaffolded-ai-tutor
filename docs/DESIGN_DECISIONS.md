| D18 | 13 Oct 2026 | Provisional RAG pipeline for the tutor: structure-aware chunks (book sections from the table of contents, summaries/contents/case studies/solutions/formula sheets excluded, ~1,000 characters), built from **cleaned** text, searched with **hybrid** retrieval (BM25 + multi-qa-MiniLM embeddings, Reciprocal Rank Fusion).
